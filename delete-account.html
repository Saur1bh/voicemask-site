Delete your VoiceMask account

VoiceMask by Moonbyte Labs

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open VoiceMask and tap Settings
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete
  3. Confirm with your Google account

If you no longer have the app

Email moonbytelabs.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my VoiceMask account", from the Google email you signed in with, and include your username. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, photo address
  • Your contacts, blocked list and voice unlocks
  • Your push notification token and sign-in account
  • Your name in past call records

What is kept

  • Anonymized call records (time, length, status). The other person still sees the call, marked "Deleted user"
  • Reports made about or by you, kept for safety
  • If someone saved you as a contact, the name in their list stays until they remove it

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Premium. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
