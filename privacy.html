Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

VoiceMask is an app for voice calls with live voice changing, made by Moonbyte Labs. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we collect

DataWhy
Google account name, email and profile photo addressTo sign you in and show your profile
Your username and display nameSo friends can find and call you. Any signed-in user can search usernames and see these, with your photo
Your contacts and blocked list in the appTo run those features. This is not your phone's address book, which the app never reads
Call records: who, when, how long, call status, voice usedCall history, safety and keeping within usage limits
Reports you make or receive, with the reasonTo prevent misuse
Push notification tokenTo ring your phone
Purchase statusTo turn on Premium. Google handles payment, and we never see your card
Usage data, crash logs and device informationTo fix problems and improve the app
Advertising IDTo show ads, unless you have Premium

Your voice

Calls are not recorded. Your voice is processed live on your phone, then sent through Agora's network to the other person during the call. We don't store audio or transcripts of calls.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, notifications, analytics and crash reports
  • Agora: carrying live call audio
  • Cloudflare: our server that creates call tokens and sends call notifications
  • Google AdMob: ads. Where required by law, you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses data
  • Google Play: payments

We don't sell your personal data. These companies may process data in other countries, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep data

  • Your account data stays until you delete your account
  • When you delete your account, your profile, contacts, blocked list and voice unlocks are deleted, and your name is removed from past call records. The other person keeps the record of that call, with "Deleted user" as the name
  • If someone saved you as a contact, the name in their list stays until they remove it, but they can't call you
  • Reports are kept for safety, so that misuse can't be escaped by deleting an account

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time, in the app or by request
  • Turn off microphone or notification permissions in Android Settings. Calls need them to work
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for corrections, at the email below
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings

Age limit

VoiceMask is only for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using it, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

We'll update this page and its date if this policy changes.
